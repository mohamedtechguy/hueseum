How it works

From a single colour to your own exhibition

  1. Choose a colour

    Use the colour picker, type a hex code, or start from a historic pigment such as ultramarine or vermilion.

  2. Refine the search

    Set how close the match should be, narrow by era and type of work, and add keywords like river or portrait.

  3. Collect what you love

    Open any work for its full label, the artist's story, and a button to hang it in your exhibition.

  4. Plan a real visit

    Hueseum checks which of your works are on the gallery walls in Chicago and builds a route card for your trip.

Why colour?

Museums sort by date and name. People remember colour.

Ask someone about a painting they saw years ago and they will often describe its colour before its title: "the blue one with the haystacks". Collection search tools rarely let you look that way.

The Art Institute's open data stores the hue, saturation and lightness of each artwork's dominant colour. Hueseum turns that data into a search you can browse with your eyes.
